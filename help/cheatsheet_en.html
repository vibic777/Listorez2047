Ready-to-use examples

  • Act number from 22 to 2167: D{1,4}.
  • Day and month without year: “27” 03 2026 г. or “27” March 2026 + DD.M → 27.03; quotes and year are omitted.
  • Full date “1 June 2026” or “01/06/2026”: DD.M.DDDD → 01.06.2026.
  • Name “ANN” or “ALEXANDER”: C+.
  • Template Act_{1}_from_{2}_{505:+1#} with number 22 produces Act_22_from_01.06.2026_505.pdf.

OCR languages: “Cyrillic (Russian)” for Russian text and “Latin” for English and other Latin-script languages. New fields inherit global settings. Empty pattern: warning. Overlapping frames: per-field Clip or Recognize. Field default and Remember last value (stores the applied value, not raw OCR) are set on the field; review offers a scan/default dropdown.
